    How to use this dashboard

    1. Start on Overview. KPI cards, key findings, areas needing attention and recommended actions summarise the current view.
    2. Filter. Choose months, categories, regions, seller tiers, fulfilment model or payment method in the bar at the top. Every chart, table, KPI, report and export follows the filters. Click a chip to remove a filter, or use Reset filters.
    3. Investigate. Each tab answers one group of questions: Product categories, Customers, Delivery, Reviews and Sellers. Hover any chart for exact values; click a month, category or region to filter to it; use ⤢ for full screen.
    4. Add data. On Add data, upload a workbook or file, read the per-table check, then apply it. Everything recalculates straight away. Undo or reset at any time.
    5. Take it away. Generate Analysis Report creates a PDF of the current or full analysis; Download Analysis Data exports the order-level data being analysed (CSV, or Excel with summaries).

    Data and its limits

    • Synthetic data: Marketvale is a fictional marketplace. Patterns come from a simulation, not from real customers or sellers.
    • Source: . Orders 1 Jan 2024 - 31 Dec 2025, extract at 31 Dec 2025 23:59. Amounts in Nigerian naira (NGN).
    • Inflation: selling prices rose about 49% over the two years, so the dashboard also shows GMV at constant January 2024 prices.
    • Open orders: orders still shipped or processing at the extract are excluded from delivery-success rates.
    • Missing delivery scans: 98 delivered orders have no delivery time; they are excluded from delivery-time and lateness measures.
    • Reviews are per order (not per product); about 58% of orders have one.
    • Associations only: tests show whether differences are larger than chance; they do not show cause and effect.
    • Where your data is kept: uploads stay in this browser's IndexedDB. Clear them with "Reset to the original dataset" or by clearing site data.
